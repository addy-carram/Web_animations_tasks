Slow

Made slowly, from clay and oak

Wood began with one question: why does a good scent need so much equipment?

Fewer things, made better

We wanted a nebulizer that looks like it belongs on a shelf, works without water and does not need a manual to enjoy. So we design every piece around two natural materials, ceramic and oak.

Each one is made by hand in a small studio. Because the work is slow, we make a few at a time and check every one before it ships.

How a Wood nebulizer is made

  1. Shape the ceramic

    The body is formed and fired by hand.

  2. Turn the oak

    The base is cut and sanded until it feels right in the hand.

  3. Assemble and test

    Every nebulizer is run before it leaves the studio.

  4. Pack and send

    Free 4 day shipping to USA & UK.

Keep it natural

Ceramic and oak age well and look better with use.

Keep it simple

One light, one mist, one switch. Nothing to fill.

Stand behind it

30 day returns and a 1 year warranty on every nebulizer.
