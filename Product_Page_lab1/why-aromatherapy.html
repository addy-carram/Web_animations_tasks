Calm

A small ritual that changes the room

Scent is the quickest way to tell your home it is time to slow down, focus or wake up.

Wind down

Soft florals and woods mark the end of the day, so your evening starts when the mist does.

Focus in

Bright, clean oils give a desk or studio its own atmosphere and make it easier to settle into work.

Freshen up

Citrus and herbs keep a room feeling open and fresh, without sprays or candles.

Five oils to start with

Start with one, add a second when you know what you like.

  • Lavender

    Soft and floral, for the evening.

  • Sweet orange

    Bright and easy, for the kitchen and the morning.

  • Eucalyptus

    Clean and cool, for a day working at home.

  • Cedarwood

    Warm and woody, a natural match for oak.

  • Peppermint

    Sharp and lively, in short bursts.
