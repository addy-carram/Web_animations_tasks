How does a waterless nebulizer work?

It turns pure essential oil into a fine mist without water, so the scent is stronger and there is no tank to refill.

Which oils can I use?

Any pure essential oil. Start with a few drops and add more if you want a stronger scent.

How do I clean it?

Unplug it and let it cool, then clean it following the care card in the box. It is designed to be easy to clean.

How long does shipping take?

Shipping is free and takes 4 days to the USA and UK.

Can I return my order?

Yes. You have 30 days to return it.

Is there a warranty?

Every nebulizer has a 1 year warranty.
